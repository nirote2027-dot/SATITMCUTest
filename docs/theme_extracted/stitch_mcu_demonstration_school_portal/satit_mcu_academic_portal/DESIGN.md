## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies an institutional yet modern academic ecosystem tailored for secondary education (Grades 7–12 / ม.1 – ม.6) within a venerable Buddhist university lineage. The system bridges royal Thai institutional dignity with high-utility digital SaaS precision. It communicates trust, diligence, intellectual rigor, and accessibility to diverse stakeholders: executives evaluating institutional KPIs, registrars generating official transcript documents (ปพ.1), teachers recording granular grade and attendance matrices, and adolescent students managing their academic pathways.

The design movement is **Corporate Modern with Soft Institutional Accents**. It relies on structured, clean data density balanced by generous whitespace, soft micro-surfaces, deliberate typographic hierarchy, and clear functional affordances. Deep lotus magenta grounds the primary identity, tempered by warm ceremonial gold accents and rigorous slate-navy typography. Visual complexity is minimized: interfaces prioritize legibility under heavy data loads (attendance rosters, transcript grids, evaluation forms) without sacrificing prestigious identity markers.

## Colors

The palette establishes a clear hierarchy between institutional branding, administrative data scaffolding, and critical academic statuses:

- **Primary Lotus Magenta (`#C2185B`, `#D81B60`):** Reserved for primary interactive controls, current active navigation states, key graduation summaries, and core brand anchoring. Lotus tint `#FCE4EC` serves as an interactive hover surface and gentle highlight.
- **Secondary Royal Gold (`#C59B27`):** Represents academic honors, GPA milestones, prestigious institutional notices, badge embellishments, and subtle divider accents.
- **Darks & Neutrals (`#0F172A`, `#1E293B`, `#64748B`, `#94A3B8`):** Deep slate-navy replaces harsh absolute blacks, conferring academic authority while significantly reducing eyestrain during prolonged grading and transcript auditing sessions.
- **Surfaces (`#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity, sterile backgrounds that ensure tabular data, student records, and PDF previews remain crisp and high in contrast.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - **Emerald Green (`#10B981` / `#ECFDF5`):** Attendance present, passed courses, completed evaluations, valid prerequisites.
  - **Amber (`#F59E0B` / `#FFFBEB`):** Warning states, pending evaluations, grade revision deadlines, border-line attendance rates (<85%).
  - **Rose Red (`#EF4444` / `#FEF2F2`):** Academic risk flag (<80% attendance, failing grade "0", incomplete status "ร" / "มส"), blocked grade gates.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes balanced bilingual typography across Thai and Latin scripts. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a clean, open geometric profile with wide apertures, ensuring distinct separation between numerals, codes, and Latin labels. When paired with standard Thai OS fallback stacks (e.g., *Sarabun*, *Noto Sans Thai*), it produces harmonious vertical cadence and baseline alignment.

Key guidelines:
- **Numerical Regularity:** In gradebooks, GPA scores, and credit tallies, utilize tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent jitter when data updates or tables render multiple decimal points (e.g., `GPAX 3.85`).
- **Administrative Clearances:** Official academic codes (e.g., ว31101, ท21102) and status indicators ("ร", "มส", "มผ") use `code-table` and `label-md` with elevated contrast to prevent confusion between similar characters.
- **Hierarchical Density:** Headers stay lean and tightly tracked to allow space for contextual filters, term selectors, and export buttons in dense navigation bars.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through **Tonal Surface Layering combined with Low-Contrast Borders and Ambient Diffuse Shadows**, creating a clean, professional workspace:

- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` (Slate Tint). The operational canvas for the entire platform.
- **Surface Level 1 (Default Containers & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp structural hairline border `1px solid #E2E8F0` and subtle ambient diffusion: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated Panels & Flyouts):** Active dropdown menus, floating action docks, and sticky table header row overlays: `0 4px 12px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Gatekeeper evaluation dialogs, ปพ.1 high-fidelity PDF print drawers, and warning modals: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over a `#0F172A` backdrop with 40% alpha opacity.

No heavy drop shadows or skeumorphic skews are permitted; interfaces rely on subtle border contrast and tone transitions to communicate nesting.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape vocabulary, communicating approachability for middle and high school students while preserving structural alignment for tabular institutional records:

- **Default Controls & Inputs:** `0.5rem` (8px) radius applied to buttons, input fields, filter dropdowns, and segmented toggles.
- **Cards & Data Surfaces:** `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) radius for dashboard cards, grade breakdown panels, and stat containers.
- **Status Badges & Avatar Chips:** Full pill radius (`9999px`) for academic standing ("ปกติ", "เฝ้าระวัง"), attendance indicators ("มา", "ขาด", "ลา"), and user chips.
- **Inner Elements:** Nested components maintain concentric geometry (e.g., an inner pill inside an 8px card uses 4px-6px radius to avoid optical distortion).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Lotus Magenta (`#C2185B`) with white text. Height: 40px (Desktop), 44px (Mobile). Hover: `#880E4F`. Active scale transform: `98%`.
- **Secondary / Academic Accent:** Bordered or soft-tinted Gold (`#C59B27`). Used for export triggers (Export ปพ.1, Print Roster) and evaluation confirmations.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Slate-800 (`#1E293B`) text on transparent background, activating to Slate-100 hover. Used for table toolbars and pagination controls.
- **Destructive:** Soft Rose background (`#FEF2F2`) with red text (`#EF4444`) for dropping course registrations or resetting disciplinary tallies.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Focus ring: `2px solid #C2185B` with 2px offset.
- **Inline Grade Input:** Compact (32px height) numeric cell inputs with centralized monospace alignment, tab-key jump indexing, and instant green/red status confirmation on blur.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- Structured with a tinted background (10-15% saturation) and a strong foreground text color:
  - **Attendance Present:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, left-dot `#10B981`.
  - **Academic Risk / Unmet Criteria:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, left-dot `#EF4444`.
  - **Special Evaluation States:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E` for pending teacher evaluations.

### Data Tables (Grades & Attendance)
- Sticky table headers with subtle bottom border (`#E2E8F0`) and solid white/slate-50 background.
- Row heights: 48px for standard dense data; 40px for bulk grade entry modes.
- Zebra striping: Alternate row shading using `#F8FAFC` on tables exceeding 20 rows.
- Right-aligned numeric columns; left-aligned student IDs and names.

### Gatekeeper Evaluation Overlay (CORE-04)
- Full-surface modal gating grade reveals. Uses an encouraging banner with Lotus-to-Gold subtle linear gradient (`135deg, #C2185B 0%, #C59B27 100%`), a clear multi-step progress bar, and locked padlock icons on blurred background grade preview tiles.

### ปพ.1 Print Preview Container (CORE-02)
- Fixed aspect-ratio canvas (A4 ratio `1:1.414`) embedded in a floating viewer. Equipped with front/back tab switching, signature field status indicators, and zoom controls.
